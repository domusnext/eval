1. The Big Picture: Weekly Meal Planning

· "Hey, robot, based on our family's calendar this week—soccer on Tuesday, parent-teacher conference on Thursday, and that potluck on Saturday—create a meal plan. Make sure Tuesday's dinner is something fast, and Saturday's dish is portable and can serve 12 people."
· "Generate a weekly meal plan that uses what we already have in the fridge. I have chicken breasts, bell peppers, zucchini, and a lot of cheddar cheese. What can we make?"
· "This week's grocery flyer says ground turkey and sweet potatoes are on sale. Build a cost-effective meal plan around those."
· "Create a meal plan with one vegetarian, one seafood, and one chicken dish this week. We need more variety."

2. Dealing with Picky Eaters & Allergies

· "My 7-year-old won't eat anything 'green' or 'mixed together.' Find me 3 recipes for dinner where vegetables are hidden or seamlessly incorporated."
· "Find a recipe for chicken tenders that is gluten-free and baked, not fried, so it's healthier for the whole family."
· "We have a nut allergy. Always double-check every recipe suggestion to ensure it's nut-free or can be easily modified."
· "Suggest one meal that everyone in the family will eat without complaining. (I know, good luck, right?)."

3. Saving Time and Effort

· "I have 30 minutes to get dinner on the table. What are the quickest recipes we can make with the ingredients we have?"
· "It's 4 PM and I forgot to take anything out for dinner. Find me recipes that use frozen chicken breasts or ground beef without a long thawing time."
· "Give me 5 'set-it-and-forget-it' slow cooker or Instant Pot recipes for this busy week."
· "Plan a 'double-duty' meal—something we can make a double batch of on Monday so we can repurpose the leftovers for Wednesday's dinner (e.g., roast chicken one night, chicken tacos another)."

4. Health & Nutrition Goals

· "We're trying to eat more whole foods. Suggest recipes that are high in protein and fiber but low in processed ingredients."
· "My doctor said I need more iron. What are some iron-rich dinner recipes the whole family might enjoy?"
· "Plan a week of balanced dinners that follow the 'MyPlate' model—lean protein, whole grains, and lots of veggies."
· "Calculate the approximate nutritional info (calories, protein, sugar) for the lasagna recipe you just suggested."

5. Specific Cravings & Situations

· "It's finally fall! Find me the best, coziest recipes for soups and stews. My family loves chili and beef stew."
· "We're having friends over for a BBQ. Generate a menu, complete with a main dish, two sides, and a dessert that I can mostly prepare ahead of time."
· "I have a craving for something like the creamy pasta from that Italian place downtown. Find me a copycat recipe."
· "It's my turn to supply the team snack for 20 kids. Find me easy, healthy, and nut-free snack ideas for after the game."

6. Leftovers & Food Waste

· "I have leftover rice, grilled salmon, and steamed broccoli from last night. What's a new recipe I can make so it doesn't go to waste?"
· "The bananas are getting too brown. What can I bake with them besides banana bread?"
· "Create a 'Use It Up' recipe based on the items in the fridge that are closest to expiring."

7. Logistics & Execution

· "For the taco salad on the meal plan, generate a precise shopping list, organized by the aisles in my local supermarket (produce, dairy, canned goods, etc.)."
· "Read me the step-by-step instructions for the meatloaf recipe while my hands are covered in flour. Guide me through it."
· "Set a timer for the cookies in the oven and remind me to put the prepped salad in the fridge."
· "Add the ingredients for this week's meal plan to my online grocery cart for pickup."
